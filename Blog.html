Why aidBridge?

15th June, 2025.

Sustainable Development Goal 2 (SDG 2), also known as "Zero Hunger," is one of the 17 goals established by the United Nations in 2015 as part of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development. aidBridge supports this goal by working to ensure that individuals affected by natural disasters have timely access to safe, nutritious food and essential resources, even in the most challenging conditions. What is SDG-2?

20th June, 2025.

Sustainable Development Goal 2 (SDG 2), known as "Zero Hunger," is one of the 17 goals established by the United Nations in 2015 as part of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development. The goal aims to end hunger, achieve food security and improved nutrition, and promote sustainable agriculture. aidBridge supports this mission by facilitating the delivery of emergency food and essential supplies to disaster-affected populations, helping restore nutrition and stability in vulnerable communities.
