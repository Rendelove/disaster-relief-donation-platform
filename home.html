LEARN MORE ABOUT SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENTAL GOAL 2 (AID BRIDGE)

What is aidBridge and it's mission ?
aidBridge aims to end hunger and malnutrition among disaster-affected communities by 2030, ensuring that all people, especially vulnerable groups like children, have access to sufficient and nutritious food year-round. The platform focuses on mobilizing donations, coordinating rapid relief, and supporting long-term recovery efforts to restore access to essential resources such as food, clean water, shelter, and healthcare.
What is Sustainable Developmental Goal 2 ?
Sustainable Development Goal 2 (SDG-2) aims to achieve "zero hunger" and is one of the 17 Sustainable Development Goals established by the United Nations in 2015. Its official wording is: "End hunger, achieve food security and improved nutrition, and promote sustainable agriculture." aidBridge supports this goal by providing a platform that channels donations to disaster-affected communities, helping ensure access to sufficient, safe, and nutritious food. Through efficient coordination of relief efforts, aidBridge contributes to reducing hunger and improving food security in crisis situations.
How does aidBridge operate ?
aidBridge operates as an intermediary platform that connects donors with disaster-affected individuals and communities in need. It facilitates donations of money and essential resources, ensuring they are securely collected and efficiently distributed through trusted local partners. aidBridge manages the verification and coordination process to guarantee that aid reaches the intended recipients quickly and transparently, helping to meet their urgent needs for food, shelter, healthcare, and other critical support.
What are the goals of aid Bridge by 2030 ?
Provide vital aid to disaster victims, including food, shelter, and healthcare. Also, build resilient communities through sustainable recovery efforts.
why donate to help disaster victims ?
Donating to help disaster victims provides vital nourishment to those in urgent need while reducing waste by rescuing and redistributing wholesome food and essential resources. Your contribution not only saves lives but also ensures that valuable aid reaches people who might otherwise go without.
